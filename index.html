FamilyFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-12

What FamilyFlow stores

FamilyFlow saves your family's schedules, children's profiles, homework, and notices in local storage on your device only. This data is not uploaded to a cloud service or third party. Device storage is not an encrypted vault — protect your device with a screen lock.

Accounts

If sign-in is configured, Firebase Authentication processes the email address and credentials you provide to create or access your account. FamilyFlow does not store your password. Calendar data is not synced between devices or to the cloud.

Location

Location sharing is off by default. The optional "preview my location" feature requests foreground permission, reads your device's location once, and clears it when you leave the screen. There is no background location service, no connected child device tracking, no location history, and no location data is uploaded anywhere.

Assistant

The in-app assistant's replies are generated entirely on-device from your own data. No external AI service (including Gemini or any other third-party model) is contacted.

Advertising and analytics

FamilyFlow does not include advertising, analytics, or child profiling of any kind.

Your controls

You can delete a child's profile and all associated calendar entries, homework, and reminders from the Settings screen at any time; this cannot be undone. Deleting your account removes your Firebase login and the workspace stored on that device. If FamilyFlow is installed on more than one device, local app data on each additional device must be cleared separately.

Data deletion requests

To request deletion of data associated with your account, use the in-app delete controls in Settings, or contact us at: anuna413@gmail.com.

Children's privacy

FamilyFlow is designed for use by parents and school staff to manage information about children. It does not knowingly collect data directly from children, does not include advertising or behavioral profiling, and stores all family data locally on the parent's or educator's device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised and, where required, users will be notified in-app.
